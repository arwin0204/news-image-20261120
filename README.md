Sum up 20 November's world news in one image — a newspaper front page for
Friday, 20 November 2026.

Lead story: COP31 enters its final day in Antalya with no deal and no mention
of fossil fuels. Also inside: the world court rules Myanmar committed genocide
against the Rohingya, the first Trump–Xi–Putin three-way summit in Shenzhen,
a Houthi missile hitting Riyadh's financial district, and six more stories.

Writes `entry.png` (1024x1024, deterministic: two runs produce identical
bytes) to the current directory, or to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set.
The image is 1024x1024 RGB, under the 1 MiB limit.
